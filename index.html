How this works

  1. Type the prompt you'd normally send.
  2. Pick "General prompt" for any task (it detects the domain and task type for you), or pick a deliverable. Each deliverable carries its own structure, expert techniques and failure modes.
  3. Set the loop: how many rounds, who critiques, and the score a draft must hit before it's allowed out.
  4. Copy the result into Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. The model drafts, scores itself against a hard rubric, gets attacked by your critique panel, and rewrites, round after round, each round on a different weakness.

Tip: the more of your own facts and opinions you put in step 03, the less the result reads like anyone else's.
